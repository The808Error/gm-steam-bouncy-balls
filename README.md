# Steam Bouncy Balls!

Basically variations of the default bouncy ball.

Variations:
Steam Happy - Gives you 10 hp
Steam Smug - Gives you 5 hp
Steam Sad - Takes away 5 hp
Steam Happy Blue - Gives you 5 armor
Steam Beatup - Does 5-25 damage
